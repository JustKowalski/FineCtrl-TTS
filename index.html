Why FineCtrl-TTS

Naturalness is more than sounding clean.

Customer-service speech must fit the role, carry the intended attitude, and place breaths, filled pauses, laughter, and emphasis exactly where they belong. Conventional quality and speaker-similarity metrics leave these behaviors largely unmeasured.

Control at every granularity

From the whole conversation
down to a single breath.

FineCtrl-TTS uses complementary control channels at different temporal scales, then evaluates each behavior with ContextualHL.
